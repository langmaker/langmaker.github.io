Language NameAttrendian
Language AuthorKevin R. VanDenBreemen
Year Began2003
SiteAttrendian
Broken LinkNo
Site LanguageEnglish
Site AuthorKevin R. VanDenBreemen
Language Typefictional language
UniquenessThis language makes use of a good deal of inflexion, providing a good deal of versatility in meaning. For example, the noun has three possible "spheres of existence" in that suffixes are added to a noun to denote its existence in the domestic, communal or natural sphere.
Language SourcesA basic knowledge of grammar in English, Latin, Esperanto, Greek.
Design PrinciplesThe design project of this language had two major goals: To improve my knowledge of linguistics and to explore, through language, the culture of the people of Attrendia.
Interest Of OthersI don't think anyone is interested in it at this time. Though I could use some tips from more experienced conlangers and linguists, if needs be.
Quotationomorop jorns' xproja tranva livoma|| osojuop taimo' sa ocorop taims' xaletran|| odistiop coms' @ jornoma etas sa ses awan|| ses eOop wanta*a||
TranslationI have undertaken many quests during my lifetime. I have experienced many sad moments and many great occasions. They are important parts of my journey and I need them. I will always need them.
