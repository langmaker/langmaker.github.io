Radilu - Conlang Profile   Advanced
Language NameRadilu
Language AuthorChris Collins
Year Began1995
SiteRadilu
Broken LinkNo
Site LanguageEnglish
Language Typepersonal language
UniquenessRadilu is designed to be complex. Chris Collins notes the following features:
  • VOS (verb-object-subject) word order
  • twenty-three noun cases with a total of 207 noun endings
  • adverb-noun agreement
  • thirteen verb tenses, four verb voices, three moods -- 1404 verb forms
  • * adjectives agree with nouns in case, number and gender
  • almost every word made up
Design PrinciplesChris Collins writes, "Radilu (meaning 'complex') is a language that I made up to have a very complicated grammar. Having seen Esperanto and other planned languages with simple grammars, I wanted to make a language that was the complete opposite. I also wanted to make Latin, which I was taking at the time, seem not so bad."
Quotationsurvate, ute fara gat i vukedute vono nu:mt, ha fa ingegovot lo munamo yuta.
TranslationGo to, let us go down, and there confound their language, that they may not understand one another's speech.
