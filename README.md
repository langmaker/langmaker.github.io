# Langmaker — Digital Preservation Project

This repository maintains a historical archive of Langmaker, a database of constructed languages, neographies, and linguistic resources.

The project is dedicated to the long-term preservation, accessibility, and usability of historical language resources within an evolving technological environment.

**Preservation and maintenance**. The project seeks to ensure the original website's continued accessibility and compatibility with contemporary and future technologies. This may require maintenance work: migration from legacy systems, adaptation to evolving web standards, restoration and maintenance of hyperlinks, reconstruction and improvement of bibliographical indexes, documentation of historical resources, bibliographical and editorial annotations, or any other actions aimed at long-term digital preservation (*archivage pérenne*, as per [CCSD–CNRS policy orientations](https://slideum.com/doc/141686/pr%C3%A9sentation-de-christine-berthaud---g%C3%A9n%C3%A9raliser-l-acc%C3%A8s-...)).

Subsequent editorial additions are clearly distinguished from the original materials.

**Transparency**. The source files and revision history are publicly accessible to facilitate scholarly consultation, technical verification, and future preservation efforts.

Maintained by the **Working Group on Language Resources and Digital Preservation** — *Colloque de la SHESL « Écriture(s) et représentations du langage et des langues »*

**Access**: [https://langmaker.github.io/](https://langmaker.github.io/)
